All-in-one AI-powered toolkit for WordPress eCommerce brands. Includes AI chatbot, product description generator, email writer, SEO assistant, and customer chat history logging.

== Description ==

SmartCommerce AI Toolkit is a powerful plugin that brings the power of AI to your WooCommerce store. It includes a variety of tools to help you automate your marketing, improve your SEO, and provide better customer support.

Features:

* **AI Chatbot:** A floating chatbot that can answer customer questions, recommend products, and more.
* **Product Content Generator:** Automatically generate product titles, short descriptions, and long descriptions.
* **Email Writer:** Write marketing emails for a variety of purposes, including abandoned cart recovery, promotions, and thank you messages.
* **SEO Assistant:** Generate SEO titles, meta descriptions, and focus keywords for your products and posts.
* **Chat Logs:** View, search, and export all chatbot conversations.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the `smartcommerce-ai` folder to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory.
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu in WordPress.
3. Go to Settings > SmartCommerce AI to configure the plugin.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

* **Do I need an OpenAI API key?**
  Yes, you need to provide your own OpenAI API key in the plugin settings.
